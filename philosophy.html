🌱 Moving Forward as a Learning Assistant and/or Eductaor

From theory → trial → implementatoin: integrating EDCS 417 into my teaching practice

📌 Reframing What Learning Is

EDCS 417 shifted my understanding of learning from content delivery to active engagement through interaction, reflection, and questioning.

Listen to forget. See to remember. Practice to understand.

🧠 Seeing Students as Resource-Rich Learners

A key shift in my thinking is recognizing that students bring cultural, social, and academic capital into every learning space.

Our role is not to over-explain content, but to connect learning to what students already know and experience.

🤝 From EDCS Student to Facilitator

Effective teaching is less about explaining and more about creating environments where students can think together.

Strategies like Think-Pair-Share, wait time, and intentional grouping redistribute participation and support equity.

❓ Questioning as Instruction

One of the most powerful tools I learned is the intentional use of questioning.

Using ideas from class like Bloom’s Taxonomy and Costa’s Levels of Questioning helps shift students from strict memorization to reasoning to understanding and being able to explain concepts themselves.

Good teaching is less about answers and more about posing the right guiding questions.

🚀 Moving Forward

As I move forward as an eductaor, I aim to consistently apply these principles:

• Promote student thinking and conceptual understanding
• Base interactions on individual strengths
• Build equitable participation structures in group work
• Apply growth mindset feedback intentionally
• Continuously reflect and adjust practice based on student responses

Teaching is not a destination — it is a continuous process of becoming.
