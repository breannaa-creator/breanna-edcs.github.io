🌱 Moving Forward as an Educator

From theory → practice → identity: integrating EDCS 417 into my teaching practice

📌 Reframing What Learning Is

EDCS 417 shifted my understanding of learning from content delivery to active meaning-making through interaction, reflection, and questioning.

Learning is not something students receive — it is something they construct.

🧠 Seeing Students as Resource-Rich Learners

A key shift in my thinking is recognizing that students bring cultural, social, and academic capital into every learning space.

My role is not to “fill gaps,” but to connect instruction to what students already know and experience.

🤝 From Instructor to Facilitator

Effective teaching is less about explaining and more about designing environments where students think together.

Strategies like Think-Pair-Share, wait time, and structured grouping redistribute participation and support equity.

❓ Questioning as Instruction

One of the most powerful tools I learned is the intentional use of questioning.

Using frameworks like Bloom’s Taxonomy and Costa’s Levels of Questioning helps shift students from memorization → reasoning → explanation.

Good teaching is less about answers and more about guiding better questions.

🚀 Moving Forward

As I move forward into future teaching spaces, I aim to consistently apply these principles:

• Design learning around student thinking, not teacher explanation
• Use strengths-based approaches in every interaction
• Build equitable participation structures in group work
• Apply growth mindset feedback intentionally
• Continuously reflect and adjust practice based on student response

Teaching is not a destination — it is a continuous process of becoming.
