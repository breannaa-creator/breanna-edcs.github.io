Applying STEM Pedagogy

Transitioning from concepts to classrooms

Think–Pair–Share

How it works:

- 1) Think individually

- 2) Team up with a group or partner

- 3) Share out loud and discuss

In the classroom:

- Used during discussions to get everyone talking

- Helped quieter students participate

Example (Calulus):

- Attempt a calculus problem individually

- Compare strategies and thought process with partner

- Analyze different approaches

Wait Time

How it works:

- Pause after asking a question

- Give students time to think and answer

In the classroom:

- We saw longer pauses which led to more thought out answers

- Students explained thinking more deeply and understood concepts

Example:

- Ask: “What conditions must be met to apply this test? Why?”

- Wait 5–10 seconds in silence. Provide additional prompts if necessary

Growth Mindset

How it works:

- Focus on effort and improvement, not just correctness

- Normalize mistakes as part of the learning process

In the classroom:

- Emphasis on students improving over time

- Shift away from “right answer only”

Example:

- Say: “Your approach improved since last time”

- Encourage revising work and learning from mistakes
