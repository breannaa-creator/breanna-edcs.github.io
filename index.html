What Does This Website Cover?

This site is a structured synthesis of the EDCS 417 STEM Pedagogy course. It brings together key theories, teaching strategies, and classroom applications from across the semester into one summarized guide.

It is designed as a quick reference + reflection tool for STEM educators, by connecting major themes from the semester like growth mindset, equity, questioning strategies, and discussion based learning learning.

📊 Course Themes (Big Picture)

Across EDCS 417, the major ideas connect into four core themes:

  • 🌱 Student-centered learning (growth mindset, strengths-based teaching)
  • 🤝 Equity & inclusion, especially in STEM spaces
  • ❓ Questioning + discussion as tools for understanding
  • 👥 Collaboration & promoting group work

Together, these core ideas support the goal of creating more accessible, engaging, and immersive STEM classrooms.
