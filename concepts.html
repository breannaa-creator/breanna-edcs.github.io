Core Concepts

🌱 Growth Mindset

Assumption: Everyone has the ability to learn. Ability grows with effort and support (“I can’t do this yet”).

Strength: Builds persistence, resilience, and long-term learning habits.

Weakness: Can ignore real barriers such as time, resources, or prior preparation.

My Teaching: Use effort-based feedback like: “That’s a strong approach—what happens if you adjust this step?”

Class connection: We focused on shifting language from correctness to process and thinking.

💪 Strength-Based Education

Assumption: Students bring valuable knowledge, experiences, and “capital” into learning spaces.

Strength: Increases confidence and student engagement by valuing existing abilities.

Weakness: Can be difficult to apply consistently across diverse learners.

My Teaching: Identify how students learn best (visual, verbal, collaborative) and build from that.

Class connection: We discussed cultural, social, and material capital in learning.

🤝 Equity & Inclusion

Assumption: Student outcomes are shaped by systems, identity, and access—not ability alone.

Strength: Promotes fairness and more inclusive participation in STEM learning.

Weakness: Complex to implement in real classroom settings and roles like Learning Assistant.

My Teaching: Encourage quieter students, balance group roles, and design inclusive participation.

Class connection: We explored “single stories” and representation in STEM.

👥 Group Work

Assumption: Learning is social and improves through collaboration.

Strength: Encourages deeper thinking and multiple problem-solving strategies.

Weakness: Participation can be uneven among group members.

My Teaching: Use Think–Pair–Share and intentionally group students by engagement.

Class connection: We practiced facilitation strategies and group structure design.

❓ Questioning

Assumption: Questions drive understanding better than direct answers.

Strength: Builds conceptual understanding and critical thinking.

Weakness: Hard to design effective questions spontaneously.

My Teaching: Use structured questioning (Costa’s Levels) and intentional wait time.

Class connection: We practiced Bloom’s Taxonomy and levels of questioning.
