Core Concepts

🌱 Growth Mindset

Assumption: Everyone has the ability to learn. Ability grows with effort and support (“I can’t do this yet”).

Strength: Builds persistence, resilience, and long-term learning habits.

Weakness: Can ignore real barriers such as time or structural inequities.

My Teaching: “That’s a strong approach—what happens if you adjust this step?”

Class connection: Shifted focus from correctness → process.

💪 Strength-Based Education

Assumption: Students bring cultural, social, and academic “capital” into learning.

Strength: Builds confidence by valuing student identity and experience.

Weakness: Hard to consistently adapt to every learner in real time.

My Teaching: Adjust explanations based on student learning style.

Class connection: Cultural / social / material capital discussion.

Listen podcast

🤝 Equity & Inclusion

Assumption: Learning outcomes are shaped by systems, not just effort.

Strength: Promotes fairness and participation across all learners.

Weakness: Difficult to fully implement in structured classroom settings.

My Teaching: Balance group roles and encourage quieter voices.

Class connection: “Single stories” in STEM education.

👥 Group Work

Assumption: Learning is social and constructed through interaction.

Strength: Encourages reasoning and collaboration.

Weakness: Unequal participation in groups.

My Teaching: Think–Pair–Share structure.

Class connection: Facilitation strategies for equity.

❓ Questioning

Assumption: Questions drive deeper thinking than answers.

Strength: Builds conceptual understanding.

Weakness: Hard to design effective questions in real time.

My Teaching: Use wait time + scaffolded questions.

Class connection: Bloom’s Taxonomy + Costa’s Levels.
