Core Concepts

🌱 Growth Mindset

Assumption: That everyone has the ability to learn. Switch from "I can't do this" to "I can't do this yet" s

Strength: Builds persistence, expands learning

Weakness: Ignores barriers. Sometimes, some things really can't be learnt.

My Teaching: Effort-based feedback. For example: "That's a great way to think about it! Try tweaking this..."

💪 Strength-Based

Assumption: Students have assets and various forms of capital

Strength: Builds confidence and helps students maximize their learning

Weakness: Hard to implementand can vary alot person to person

My Teaching: Focus on strengths. Question whether they learn visually, audibly...

🤝 Equity & Inclusion

Assumption: Systems and ubringing impact learning

Strength: Promotes fairness, aims to provide equal opportunity

Weakness: Complex to apply especially in the role of a Learning Assistant

My Teaching: Encourage inclusive groups and quiet students to pitch in

👥 Group Work

Assumption: Learning is social

Strength: Engages students and forces them to think deeply about their understanding

Weakness: Unequal participation - more difficult for introverted students

My Teaching: Think-Pair-Share. Recognize students on the same problem and promote groupwork

❓ Questioning

Assumption: Questions drive thinking, implements ideas much better than giving an answer

Strength: Deep understanding of the concept - not just memorizing the problem

Weakness: Difficult to come up with on the spot and often caters to different learning styles

My Teaching: Practice forming guiding questions and use wait time
