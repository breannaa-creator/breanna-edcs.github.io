Core Concepts

🌱 Growth Mindset

Assumption: Everyone has the ability to learn, through effort and support.

Strength: Builds persistence, resilience, and long-term learning habits.

Weakness: May ignore real barriers such as time inequalities. Some things can't be fully learnt.

My Teaching: Shift from "I can't do this" to "I can't do this yet."

Class connection: Focus shifted from being automatically correct to recognizing learning as a process.

💪 Strength-Based Education

Assumption: Students bring cultural, social, and academic “capital” into learning.

Strength: Builds confidence by valuing student connections and experience.

Weakness: Hard to quickly adapt to every learner in real time.

My Teaching: Adjust explanations based on student learning style (visual, auditory...).

Class connection: Cultural / social / material capital discussion.

🤝 Equity & Inclusion

Assumption: Learning outcomes are shaped by the system, not purely effort.

Strength: Promotes fairness and participation across all learners.

Weakness: Difficult to fully implement in a classroom, especially as an LA.

My Teaching: Balance group roles when pairing students and encourage quieter voices.

Class connection: Recognizing restraints such as technology, time, ability to prepare...

👥 Group Work

Assumption: Learning is social and discussion generates understanding.

Strength: Encourages reasoning and collaboration between peers.

Weakness: Unequal participation in groups (introverion, students lacking effort).

My Teaching: Think–Pair–Share structure.

Class connection: Strategies that promote thinking and discussion (wait time, think pair share).

❓ Questioning

Assumption: Questions drive deeper thinking than providing immediate answers.

Strength: Builds conceptual understanding and constructs a thinking pathway.

Weakness: Hard to design effective questions on the spot.

My Teaching: Use wait time plus levels of questioning starting with high level questioning.

Class connection: Bloom’s Taxonomy + Costa’s Levels.
